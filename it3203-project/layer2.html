Layer 2 Solutions

A Layer 2 (L2) is a separate network built on top of a Layer 1. It handles transactions off the base chain, then posts compressed data back to the Layer 1. Users get lower fees and faster confirmations, and the Layer 1 still acts as the final judge.

Rollups

Rollups are the main Layer 2 design on Ethereum. They execute many transactions, bundle them into a batch, and publish the batch data to Ethereum. In March 2024, Ethereum’s Dencun upgrade added “blobs” (EIP-4844), a cheaper way for rollups to post data, which lowered Layer 2 fees.

Optimistic vs. Zero-Knowledge Rollups

The two main rollup types
FeatureOptimistic rollupZK rollup
How it proves correctnessAssumes batches are valid; anyone can submit a fraud proof during a challenge windowPosts a cryptographic validity proof with each batch
Withdrawals to Layer 1Slower, because of the challenge window (about a week on many networks)Faster, once the proof is verified
ComplexitySimpler, closer to the original EVMMore complex math and tooling
ExamplesArbitrum, OP Mainnet, BasezkSync Era, Starknet, Scroll

Other Layer 2 Approaches

  • State channels: Bitcoin’s Lightning Network lets two parties transact off-chain and settle on-chain later.
  • Sidechains: networks such as Polygon PoS run their own validators, so they do not inherit full Layer 1 security. Many people treat them as a separate category from rollups.

Tradeoffs to Know

  • Moving funds between layers (bridging) adds steps and risk.
  • Some rollups still rely on a single sequencer to order transactions.
  • Liquidity and apps are spread across many separate Layer 2 networks.
